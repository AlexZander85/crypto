The problem

Why beginners lose their deposit.

Not bad luck. Three recurring mistakes — everyone makes them.

🎲 Betting on luck

You buy on hype from a chat. There is nothing to check before entry — you don't know what to check.

⚡ Leverage until liquidation

x10 leverage turns a 10% price drop into minus one hundred percent of your deposit. Most people learn their liquidation price after the fact.

😱 Emotions instead of a system

Fear sells at the bottom. Greed buys at the top. Without a system, you supply liquidity to those who have one.
